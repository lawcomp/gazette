About Law Companion Gazette

Law Companion Gazette is where law students share what they have learned. Articles are written by students and cover Nigerian law: contract, constitutional law, decided cases, criminal law, tort, commercial law, jurisprudence and legislation.

How posts are published

Every post is checked and then reviewed by the Law Companion team. Only posts that are accepted are gazetted here. Law Wiki articles can be improved by other students, and every change is reviewed before it goes live.

Write for us

Create an account and subscribe in the Law Companion app, then log in on this site to write. Your name, institution and short bio appear under your post.

Corrections

Found a mistake? Open the post and press Suggest a correction. You need to be logged in.

Please note

Posts are written by students for learning. They are not legal advice. Contact Law Companion through the app.
